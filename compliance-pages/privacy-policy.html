Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Overview

PixelRNG provides private SMS status alerts for backend readiness and operational health. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected for those alerts and how it is used.

Information We Collect

For SMS alerts, PixelRNG may collect:

  • Your mobile phone number.
  • Your SMS opt-in and opt-out status.
  • Basic delivery information from the SMS provider, such as message status and timestamps.

How We Use Information

We use this information only to send private PixelRNG operational SMS alerts, manage opt-in and opt-out requests, troubleshoot delivery, and maintain compliance records.

SMS Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

SMS delivery requires use of service providers such as carriers and messaging platforms. These providers process messages only as needed to deliver the alert service.

Your Choices

You can unsubscribe from PixelRNG SMS alerts at any time by replying STOP. You can request help by replying HELP.

Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or PixelRNG SMS alerts, use the contact method provided on the site where these SMS alert pages are hosted.
